About Me
I am Hsin-Chi Chung, a graduate of the Department of Biomedical Sciences and Engineering at National Central University. During my master's I focused on NLP and machine learning: my thesis processed 810,000+ real emergency department records, and through feature engineering and model optimization I raised prediction AUC by 9.6%, building solid data engineering and statistical modeling skills.

After graduating I moved into hands-on LLM and generative AI work: I built an emergency medical record retrieval and Q&A system on a RAG architecture, validated with RAGAS to reach Context Recall 0.89. I also delivered a LINE × Gemini chatbot end to end on my own — architecture design, API integration, zero-cost deployment and the reliability engineering to keep it running in production.

As an undergraduate I spent nearly two years as a research assistant interning at a hospital AI Center, where I led development of a healthcare-associated infection surveillance system that went live for clinical staff and cut manual monitoring workload by 97%. It took first place in the university capstone competition and was selected for an international conference exhibition in Seoul. I am now actively looking for a full-time role where I can keep combining research rigor with engineering execution to build products people actually use.
