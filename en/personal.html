Life Outside the Lab
🎉 Student Association · Events Director
As events director, I planned and ran every department event. Timing, headcount, atmosphere, purpose — every detail had to be thought through, so the department felt closer.
🏀 Department Basketball Team · Captain
As captain of the department basketball team, I led us to third place in an inter-school tournament two years running. It was never only about playing — it meant getting teammates to train seriously and to work for each other on court. Basketball is where I learned teamwork and leadership.
🌅 Family · Memories
Sunset at Alishan

Watching the sunset at Alishan with my family. We were up at four or five in the morning to catch the Alishan forest railway — the kind of view everyone should see at least once.
Picking Pomelos Back Home

My grandparents are getting on in years and can no longer climb the pomelo trees, so every year the younger ones go back to help bring the fruit down. The pomelos are really just the excuse — what I want is a reason to go home, see them, and spend time with them.
✈️ Japan · Graduation Trip
A graduation trip to Japan with my master's classmates. A chance to travel abroad, unwind and shake off the pressure — time I still treasure.
